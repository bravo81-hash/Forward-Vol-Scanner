Framework reference

Three postures, one surface

The strategy label is the final step. Price action determines the decision boundary; volatility determines whether the proposed shape is paid appropriately.

V14 · NEUTRAL DEFENCE

All-weather protected BWB

Broken-wing put butterfly with a separate long-tail put. Favoured when stressed IV, unstable gamma and high ATR make resilience the priority.

V17 · BULLISH POSTURE

Opportunistic protected BWB

Bullish BWB whose upper expiration plateau must remain above zero after executable pricing. Support or breakout confirmation is compulsory.

V22 · VOLATILITY PLAY

Adaptive fly + long call

Symmetrical put fly with an approximately 75Δ ITM call. Wing width follows the IV expected move and is reconsidered after persistent skew change.

01 · Enter V17Support holds or a valid breakout confirms the bullish posture.
02 · Model V22Consolidation or a material IV rise changes the expression, not automatically the trade.
03 · Defend with V14High ATR and unstable volatility justify the structurally defensive translation.
Important boundary

Transparent, not canonical

The source blueprint does not publish enough mechanical detail to recreate Locke’s exact strike algorithm. The live builder therefore exposes every assumption instead of hiding it behind a “confidence” score.

  • 77 DTE is targeted from currently listed Friday expiries, with a 30–85 DTE fallback.
  • Expected move uses the selected expiry’s ATM IV; skew comes from live 25Δ option IV.
  • Exact legs are repriced at TWS NBBO with option-level liquidity checks.
  • Every result remains a hypothesis for ONE/OptionNet validation; live order staging is disabled.
